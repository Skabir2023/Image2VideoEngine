## The revelation (beat 4)

`FACTCHECK.md` carries the pair that makes this a revelation rather than a statistic: NHRA
F/Stock demanded **500** identical cars, and AMC built **1,512**. The film's fourth beat is the
gap between the two — the homologation special that was never really special, because the factory
made three times the number racing required. It reframes the whole film: everything before it is
“the car that had to exist,” and this is “the car that overshot.”

A statistic would have been “1,512 built.” The revelation is “500 were required.”

## Future stories — three angles on this same car

These are what stop a Part 2 from repeating this film's hook and fact. One per post, never two
inside 90 days, and never this film's Secret Story again.

- **Size / Looks** — the A-scheme paint: a red-and-blue stripe package drawn for a car that was
  only supposed to be a homologation special, and what it looks like next to a plain Rogue.
- **Engine / Speed** — the 390 and the Borg-Warner T-10: 315 hp and 425 lb-ft in a compact body,
  and what a 14.2-second quarter-mile meant against the 1969 field.
- **Why It Failed** — AMC killed the SC/Rambler after one model year and folded the name into the
  Hornet; this film's production numbers are the setup, not the story.
