# Prompts — 1969 AMC SC/Rambler

The full prompt for each plate is the identity block from `PLATES.md`, the framing clause, and the fixed studio tail. They are repeated here so the image batch can be audited without opening the table.

Shared prefix: `Create one photorealistic photograph, vertical portrait 9:16: `
Shared tail: `empty concrete studio, golden hour light, 35mm, no people, no animals, no text, no watermark.`

1. `01_hero_front34.png` — Front three-quarter, car facing camera, full car in frame, hood scoop visible.
2. `02_side.png` — Side profile, full car in frame, red and blue stripe running nose to tail.
3. `03_rear.png` — Rear three-quarter, black tail panel, chrome bumper, twin exhaust tips.
4. `04_engine.png` — Hood lifted, engine bay filling the frame: 390 V8, four-barrel carburetor, ram-air cleaner.
5. `05_interior.png` — Interior through the open door: gray vinyl buckets, Sun tachometer on the steering column, Hurst T-handle shifter.
6. `06_wheel.png` — Tight front wheel close-up: Magnum 500 wheel, chrome trim ring, blackwall tire.
7. `07_scoop.png` — Low front close-up of the ram-air hood scoop and black grille, red and blue stripes.
8. `08_tail.png` — Rear lower close-up: chrome bumper, twin exhaust tips, black tail panel.

The exact prompts are recorded in each `.images.json` beside the finished plate.
