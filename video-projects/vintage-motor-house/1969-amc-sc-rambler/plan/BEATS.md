# Beats — 1969 AMC SC/Rambler

Eleven plates, eleven scenes, 30.000 s, 1800 frames. A fresh plate every 2.2–3.25 s — inside the
3 s cadence, with the six fixed angles first (hero, side, rear, engine bay, interior, wheel) and
five close-ups after them, each on a beat of its own. No plate is used twice.

Every cut lands 30–65 ms before the first word of the next line, in a measured pause of the take.
No text reveal starts before its word. Only one number is on screen at a time, and it is the one
being spoken. The closing card holds alone from the last word (28.00) to the end, with the
big-block roar answering it — the last 2 s of picture carry no narration.
