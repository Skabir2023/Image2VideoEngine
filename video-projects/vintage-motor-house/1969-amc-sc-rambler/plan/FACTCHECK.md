# Fact check — 1969 AMC SC/Rambler

All figures below are used in the film or caption. No horsepower, speed or production number is invented.

| Claim | Verified value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model year and name | 1969 AMC Hurst SC/Rambler, based on the two-door Rambler Rogue hardtop | Hemmings, “Rambler Swan Song”; Old Cars Weekly |
| Engine | 390 cu in OHV V8, 315 hp, 425 lb-ft | Hemmings; HowStuffWorks |
| Price | $2,998 base | Hemmings; HowStuffWorks; NAPA Know How |
| 0–60 | 6.3 s | HowStuffWorks; Street Muscle Magazine |
| Quarter-mile | 14.2–14.3 s at about 99–100 mph | Car Life test via Curbside Classic: 14.20 at 100.8; HowStuffWorks: 14.3 at 99 |
| Production | 1,512 built | HowStuffWorks; Hemmings; 1512 Registry |
| Homologation | NHRA F/Stock required 500 identical cars; AMC planned 500 | Over-Drive Magazine; Car Life intro |
| 1,512 against 500 | 1,512 / 500 = 3.02 — “three times what the rule demanded” is arithmetic on the two sourced rows above, not a new claim | derived |
| Gearbox | Borg-Warner T-10 four-speed with Hurst shifter | Hemmings; HowStuffWorks |
| Paint | White with red/blue “A” scheme on most cars | Hemmings; Muscle Car Club |

## Checked and left out

- The 325-hp figure in one Old Cars Weekly article conflicts with the factory 315-hp rating used by Hemmings and HowStuffWorks. The film uses **315**.
- The 12.07-second quarter-mile in Over-Drive is a racer’s modified/street-tire result, not a stock test. The film uses the **14.2** stock figure.
- “Giant killer” is a characterization, not a number; the caption says “right with cars that cost far more,” which the price and trap speed support.
- The quarter-mile (14.2 s at 100.8 mph, Car Life) is sourced and was used in the first cut; the
  30-second script dropped it for the homologation story, so it appears in neither the narration
  nor the caption. It stays here for a Part 2.
- No top-speed claim is made.
- No claim that every car had the red/white/blue paint; the film’s plates use the A-scheme because it is the signature livery.

## Verdict

**0 invented facts.** The script’s six numeric claims — 500 identical cars, 315 hp, 390 cu in,
$2,998, 6.3 s to 60, 1,512 built — all trace to the sources above, and the seventh figure the
voice says (“three times”) is division on two of them. The caption repeats only those numbers,
plus the Borg-Warner T-10 / Hurst shifter row, which the film does not say.
