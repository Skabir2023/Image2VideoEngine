## Hook
"BMW built the M1 to go racing, and the rules changed before the first car was finished."

## Why this angle and not another
- **Not Price / Rarity** — the Daytona and the Mustang already sold "how few were built".
- **Not Engine / Speed** — the GNX owns the horsepower beat in this run.
- **Why It Failed** is the one angle that *needs* this car's specific history (Lamborghini, the
  rule change, the five-factory build), and it ends on a genuine twist: BMW invented a one-make
  series so the car could race at all. That gives the film its payoff without inventing anything.
