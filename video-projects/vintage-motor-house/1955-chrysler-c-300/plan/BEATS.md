# Beats — 1955 Chrysler C-300

Eight plates, eight scenes, 20.400 s, 1224 frames. A fresh plate every 1.73–3.20 s — inside the
3 s cadence (master §7, gate tolerance 3.25 s), with the six fixed angles first (hero, side, rear,
engine bay, interior, wheel) and two close-ups after them (grille, taillight), each on a beat of
its own. No plate is used twice.

Every cut lands in a measured pause of the take — 50–65 ms before the next word, except the first,
which takes the long 0.89 s pause at 3.20 s so the hero plate stays inside 3.20 s.
No text reveal starts before its word. Only one number is on screen at a time, and it is the one
being spoken: `300` (horsepower), `127` (Daytona), then `300` again as the car's name — the same
digits doing two jobs is the joke the film is built on. `331` and `1955` stay in the voice and the
kicker; they are not cards.

The closing card holds alone from the last word (18.41) to the end, with the Hemi roar answering
it — the last 2 s of picture carry no narration.
