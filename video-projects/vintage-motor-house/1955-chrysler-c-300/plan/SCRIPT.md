# Script — 1955 Chrysler C-300

**1955 Chrysler C-300: The Luxury Coupe That Won NASCAR** · Engine / Speed · 43 words · film length measured from the take

## The read, as one paragraph

America's most powerful car in 1955 was a Chrysler luxury coupe. 300 horsepower, from a 331 Hemi. At Daytona it hit 127 and won the NASCAR title. Then Chrysler named the car after its own horsepower. Would you take one with two gears?

One chain, not a fact list. The first line makes a claim nobody expects; the second explains how
(a Hemi); the power then has to go somewhere, so it goes to Daytona, where it wins; the win makes
the name — the model number *is* the horsepower — and the last line turns the whole thing on the
one thing that should not work: two gears. Every sentence is the reason for the next, and the
question is the argument the comments will have.

Word count: **43**. Every number is sourced in `FACTCHECK.md`: 1955, 300 hp, 331 cu in, 127 mph,
NASCAR title, PowerFlite's two ratios. Nothing invented; "most powerful car in 1955" is the
highest US rating that year (Corvette 195, Thunderbird 198, Eldorado 270).
