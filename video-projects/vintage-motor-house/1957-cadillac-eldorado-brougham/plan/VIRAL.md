# Eldorado Brougham — viral score (Step 2 of the reel workflow)

Hook: **"Cadillac built just 704 of these — and each one was finished by hand."**

## Score

| Axis | Max | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook shock (first 3 s) | 25 | 20 | 704 is small enough to sound like a mistake, and the film opens on the engine before any words. Short of a jaw-drop: it is a number, not a stunt. |
| Broad relatability | 20 | 18 | Rarity, money and luxury need no car knowledge. No engine codes, no jargon. |
| Curiosity gap | 20 | 16 | "Only 704?" pulls you in, but the next line answers it, so the unanswered question is short-lived. |
| Shareability / comment bait | 15 | 13 | The six magnetic cups is the detail people repeat, and the close asks out loud which detail you would keep — a choice, not a quiz, so answering is one word. Still a niche car. |
| Visual wow | 20 | 16 | Seven plates, hard warehouse light, headlamps lit on the close. Same car in the same building throughout, which caps it. |
| **Total** | **100** | **83** | **Passes the 80 gate.** |

Weakest axis is hook shock. If a sharper first line is wanted, swap the open to the price
("$13,074 in 1957 — more than a Rolls-Royce") and rescore; that trades the rarity hook for a
money hook, which usually reads wider.

## Rejection list

None recorded for this project: no alternative cars were scored before this one was built, so
there is nothing that was rejected and nothing to show as filtered. The gate was applied to this
car only, and it clears it at 83.

## Why this one travels

- **A number people do not believe.** "704" is small enough to feel like a mistake, which
  is exactly what makes someone stop and check the caption.
- **A detail worth telling someone else.** Six magnetic cups in the glovebox is the kind of
  fact that gets repeated in a comment. The film gives it a full beat and a label.
- **A price that reframes the car.** $13,074 in 1957, more than a Rolls-Royce, turns a
  pretty Cadillac into a statement about what America thought it was.
- **A sound before a word.** Starting on the engine with nobody talking buys two seconds of
  attention, which is the scarcest thing on a feed.
- **Clean enough to loop.** 20.0 s, one read, a closing roar and a fade — it replays without
  feeling like it restarted.

Comment bait that is honest: the spoken "roof, or glove box, which would you keep?" and "704 built — how
many are left?"
