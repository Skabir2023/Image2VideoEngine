## Flow test

Read as one breath it is a chain: *503 → because a rule said 500 → so Dodge did the minimum →
and the minimum needed a wing → the wing sat 23 inches up (not for the trunk) → for clean air →
the nose and the window too → and that shape did 200 → and then nobody bought them → would you
drive it?* Each sentence answers the question the one before it opened. Delete any sentence and
the next one loses its reason: without "500 or stay home" the 503 is trivia; without the 23
inches the 200 is a boast; without the clean-air correction the wing is just a spoiler; without
the dealer lots the rule was worth it. The last line still lands straight after the first, which
is why the middle is not padding.
