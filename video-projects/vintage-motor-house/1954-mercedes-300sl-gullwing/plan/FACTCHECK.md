# Fact-check — 1954 Mercedes-Benz 300SL Gullwing

Every number in the script, where it comes from, and whether I could confirm it.
Meta AI proposed the facts; I re-checked each one against the sources below before
anything was written into a script. Nothing here is invented.

| Claim | Value | Source (Meta) |
|---|---|---|
| production | 1,400 built | https://www.supercars.net/blog/1954-mercedes-benz-300-sl-gullwing |
| power | 215 horsepower | https://www.supercars.net/blog/1954-mercedes-benz-300-sl-gullwing |
| top speed | 161 mph | https://www.supercars.net/blog/1954- mercedes-benz-300-sl-gullwing |
| top speed | 250 km/h standard axle, 260 km/h with 3.25 axle (=161 mph) | https://www.uniquecarsandparts.com/mercedes_300SL_technical_specifications.htm |
| first | first production car with direct fuel injection | https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/innovation/milestones/mercedes-benz-300-sl-gullwing-w-198 |

## Verdict
All figures confirmed. Two notes:
- 215 hp is the DIN rating (240 hp by the older SAE gross measure). The script uses DIN,
  which is what Mercedes published and what every spec sheet repeats.
- Speeds are quoted in the unit the source states, not converted on screen.

No fact in the script lacks a line in this table.
