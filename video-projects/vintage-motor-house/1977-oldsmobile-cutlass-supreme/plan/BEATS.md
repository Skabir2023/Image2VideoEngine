## The chains the film is built on

- **Hook → setup.** "In 1977" is a date, not a claim, so the shock waits one scene and lands on the
  noun: *hid a Chevrolet*. The card shows the half the voice has not said yet, `HID A / CHEVY`, on
  the words *hid* (3.84) and *Chevrolet* (4.14).
- **Setup → turn.** "They paid for a Rocket 350" creates the thing that was taken away, so
  *found a Chevy* has something to betray. The engine bay carries two sentences because the belt is
  the proof of the engine and no silence separates them.
- **Turn → revelation.** The belt is a physical detail a viewer can picture, which forces the
  question *how did nobody notice* — answered by the paint. That is the one idea in the film the
  audience does not already know.
- **Revelation → proof.** "Nobody could tell" is a two-word turn of the screw on an anonymous
  wheel, then the verdict: 14,000 owners, $550 each, both counted out with `TX.roll`.
- **Proof → question.** The question lands on the badge the buyer trusted, and the car answers it
  in the tail with two seconds of engine and no words.

The next word after each cut starts 0.04–0.31 s later, so the picture always changes in the pause.
The last cut is after the final word, which is what gives the closing plate the roar alone.

`s04-engine` and `s07-valvecover` are the longest stills at 3.083 s — inside the 3.25 s ceiling.
The shortest is `s06-wheel` at 1.367 s, because "Nobody could tell." is a 0.85 s sentence and the
cut before it may not move: there is no silence between "blue." and "Nobody" to cut in.
